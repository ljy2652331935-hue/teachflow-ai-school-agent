## 3. Recommended Teaching Intervention

The teacher should not start with the formula again.

Recommended flow:

1. Start with a curiosity hook:
   "Why can a song, an image, and a quantum wave all be understood through frequencies?"

2. Use a prism analogy:
   A prism splits white light into colors.
   Fourier Transform splits a complex signal into frequencies.

3. Show a visual explanation:
   Complex waveform -> Fourier Transform prism -> simple sine waves.

4. Then introduce the formal definition:
   Time domain shows how the signal changes over time.
   Frequency domain shows what frequencies build the signal.

5. Finish with a micro-quiz to test understanding.

## 4. Differentiated Learning Materials

### Level 1: Confused Students

Students:
S001, S004, S005, S007

Explanation:
Imagine listening to a song. It sounds like one complex sound, but inside it there are many notes and frequencies mixed together. Fourier Transform helps us separate the sound and see what frequencies are inside.

Analogy:
Fourier Transform is like a prism. A prism splits white light into colors. Fourier Transform splits a complex signal into frequencies.

Task:
Explain Fourier Transform using the prism analogy.

---

### Level 2: Partially Understood Students

Students:
S002, S003

Explanation:
In the time domain, we see how a signal changes over time. In the frequency domain, we see what frequencies make up the signal. Fourier Transform is the bridge between these two views.

Task:
Draw a simple diagram showing time domain on the left, Fourier Transform in the middle, and frequency domain on the right.

---

### Level 3: Ready to Apply Students

Students:
S006, S008

Challenge:
