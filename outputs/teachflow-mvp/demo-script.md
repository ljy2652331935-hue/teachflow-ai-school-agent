# TeachFlow 2-Minute Demo Script

## Positioning

TeachFlow is not an AI tutor. It is an AI teaching control system. It helps teachers discover what students truly do not understand, generate differentiated interventions, and publish them only after teacher approval.

## Demo Flow

1. Open Teacher Dashboard.
2. Show the Fourier Transform demo case: lesson note, learning objectives, quiz responses, and current lesson plan.
3. Click Run TeachFlow Agent.
4. Open Misconception Map and show evidence from student answers.
5. Open Differentiated Materials and show Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 interventions.
6. Open Teacher Control Panel and preview as student.
7. Approve the intervention.
8. Publish to Student Portal.
9. Submit the mini quiz from the student view.
10. Show Understanding Profile Updated and the Audit Log.

## Closing Line

AI accelerates lesson diagnosis and intervention design, but the teacher keeps control over approval, publishing, and rollback.
